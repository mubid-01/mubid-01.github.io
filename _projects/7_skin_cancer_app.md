I fine-tuned EfficientNet models (B0–B4) on the HAM10000 dataset to classify seven types of skin lesions from dermoscopic images, reaching 87.91% accuracy and an 87% F1-score, and packaged the model as an app.

**Tools:** Python, TensorFlow, Streamlit, Flutter
