AeroIOP is a portable, non-contact tonometer for measuring intraocular pressure. It uses a calibrated air-puff mechanism with laser and photodetector sensing, and converts the sensor response to pressure through regression-based calibration. The estimated cost is about $180, compared with more than $750 for commercial devices.

**Tools:** SolidWorks, Arduino, Python
