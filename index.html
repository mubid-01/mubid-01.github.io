Md. Mehedi Hassan

Research Engineer, mHealth Lab, Department of Biomedical Engineering, Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET)

Dhaka, Bangladesh

m.hassan.bme [at] gmail.com

I am a Research Engineer at the mHealth Lab, Department of Biomedical Engineering, Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), working with Dr. Taufiq Hasan. I completed my B.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering at BUET in June 2026.

My research focuses on making machine learning for medical imaging reliable under real-world conditions, where clinical inputs can be missing or mistyped, labels are scarce, and data shift across scanners, sites and populations. I have worked on fetal ultrasound, retinal imaging, brain MRI and cough audio, with an emphasis on interpretable models and honest evaluation.

I collaborate with the Department of International Health at Johns Hopkins University and the Projahnmo Research Foundation on gestational age estimation from fetal ultrasound in rural Bangladesh, and with the Institute of Global Health at the University of Geneva on evaluating machine learning for mpox diagnosis. I am applying to PhD programs starting in Fall 2027.

Research interests

  • Trustworthy and interpretable machine learning for healthcare
  • Robustness to distribution shift and unreliable inputs
  • Multimodal learning with imaging and clinical data
  • Shortcut learning and spurious correlations
  • Medical AI for resource-limited settings

selected publications

  1. CIBM
    Context-Aware Asymmetric Ensembling for Interpretable Retinopathy of Prematurity Screening via Active Query and Vascular Attention
    Md. Mehedi Hassan and Taufiq Hasan
    Computers in Biology and Medicine, 2026
    Abs DOI arXiv Bib Code

    Retinopathy of Prematurity (ROP) is among the major causes of preventable childhood blindness. Automated screening remains challenging, primarily due to limited data availability and the complexity of the condition involving both structural staging and microvascular abnormalities. We propose the Context-Aware Asymmetric Ensemble (CAA Ensemble), which simulates clinical reasoning through two specialized streams: a Multi-Scale Active Query Network (MS-AQNet) that uses clinical context as dynamic query vectors to guide localization of the fibrovascular ridge, and VascuMIL, which encodes Vascular Topology Maps within a gated multiple instance learning network to identify vascular tortuosity. Tested on a highly imbalanced cohort of 188 infants (6004 images), the framework achieved a Macro F1-score of 0.93 for broad ROP staging and an AUC of 0.996 for Plus Disease detection, with counterfactual attention heatmaps and vascular threat maps for interpretability.

  2. arXiv
    SYNAPSE-Net: A Unified Framework with Lesion-Aware Hierarchical Gating for Robust Segmentation of Heterogeneous Brain Lesions
    Md. Mehedi Hassan, Shafqat Alam, Shahriar Ahmed Seam, and Maruf Ahmed
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2510.26961 (under review), 2025
    Abs arXiv Bib Code

    Automatic segmentation of diverse heterogeneous brain lesions using multi-modal MRI is challenging, mainly because of the limited generalizability and high prediction variance of pathology-specific deep learning models. We propose SYNAPSE-Net, a unified multi-stream framework combining convolutional encoders, Swin Transformer global context modeling, cross-modal attention fusion and a hierarchical gated decoder, trained with a variance-aware strategy. Validated on WMH MICCAI 2017, ISLES 2022 and BraTS 2020, it achieved a DSC of 0.831 and HD95 of 3.03 on WMH, the lowest HD95 of 9.69 on ISLES 2022, and the highest tumor core DSC of 0.8651 on BraTS 2020.

  3. An Integrated Edge-AI Framework for Interpretable Respiratory Screening via Adaptive Cough Analysis in Low-Resource Settings
    Md. Mehedi Hassan, Fariha Hasan Chowdhury, Sumaiya Islam Shotota, and Nafisa Akter
    In 2nd International Conference on Advances in Computing, Communication, Electrical, and Smart Systems (accepted), 2026
    Abs Bib

    We propose a framework for classifying pneumonia, pertussis, bronchitis and healthy coughs. A dual-threshold hysteresis gating technique isolates cough events from noise, which are converted into log-Mel spectrograms for an EfficientNet-B1 classifier. Using nested, subject-aware cross-validation on 426 participants, the system achieved AUCs of 1.00 for healthy controls, 0.99 for pertussis, 0.94 for bronchitis and 0.92 for pneumonia, with 85% patient-level accuracy. The pipeline runs fully offline in an Android application.
